🍽️ Normal Diet

Balanced meals with all food groups for general nutrition and wellness

About This Diet

Normal Diet is a balanced approach to nutrition that incorporates all major food groups in appropriate proportions. It's designed for individuals without specific dietary restrictions and promotes overall health and wellness.

Key Features:

  • ✓ Balanced proteins, carbohydrates, and fats
  • ✓ Rich in fruits and vegetables
  • ✓ Includes whole grains and fiber
  • ✓ Variety of food sources
  • ✓ Adequate micronutrients and minerals

Health Benefits

  • 💪 Muscle Health
    Adequate protein supports muscle maintenance and growth
  • 🧠 Brain Function
    Balanced nutrients support cognitive performance
  • ❤️ Heart Health
    Variety of foods promotes cardiovascular wellness
  • 🛡️ Immunity
    Diverse nutrients strengthen immune system

💡 Health Tips & Guidelines

Meal Timing
Eat 3 main meals with 2 healthy snacks for optimal nutrition
Portion Control
Use balanced plate method: 1/2 vegetables, 1/4 protein, 1/4 grains
Hydration
Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily for optimal body function
Food Variety
Include different colors in your plate for diverse nutrients
