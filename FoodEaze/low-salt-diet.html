🧂 Low Salt Diet

Minimal sodium content to support heart and kidney health

About This Diet

The Low Salt Diet is designed to reduce sodium intake, which helps manage blood pressure and reduce the risk of cardiovascular diseases. This diet focuses on fresh, unprocessed foods with minimal added salt while maintaining nutritional balance.

Key Features:

  • ✓ Reduced sodium content
  • ✓ Fresh, whole foods
  • ✓ Herb and spice flavoring
  • ✓ Limited processed foods
  • ✓ Natural ingredient focus

Health Benefits

  • ❤️ Blood Pressure Control
    Reduces hypertension and related risks
  • 🫘 Kidney Health
    Protects kidney function and reduces strain
  • 💧 Fluid Balance
    Helps maintain healthy fluid retention
  • 🫀 Vascular Health
    Supports healthy blood vessels

💡 Health Tips & Guidelines

🌿 Use Fresh Herbs
Add flavor with basil, oregano, and garlic instead of salt
📦 Read Labels
Check sodium content in packaged foods carefully
🚫 Avoid Processed
Limit canned and pre-packaged foods high in sodium
💧 Increase Potassium
Include bananas, potatoes, and spinach for balance
