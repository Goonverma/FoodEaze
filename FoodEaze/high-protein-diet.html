💪 High Protein Diet

Protein-rich meals for muscle maintenance and faster recovery

About This Diet

The High Protein Diet emphasizes protein-rich foods to support muscle development, repair, and recovery. Ideal for post-surgery patients, athletes, and those with high protein requirements for healing and strength maintenance.

Key Features:

  • ✓ High protein content
  • ✓ Lean meat and fish
  • ✓ Dairy and legumes
  • ✓ Balanced nutrients
  • ✓ Muscle support focus

Health Benefits

  • 💪 Muscle Recovery
    Accelerates healing and muscle repair
  • 🦴 Bone Strength
    Supports bone density and structure
  • 🧬 Immune Support
    Strengthens immune system function
  • ⚡ Sustained Energy
    Provides long-lasting energy throughout day

💡 Health Tips & Guidelines

🍗 Quality Proteins
Choose lean meat, fish, eggs, and plant-based proteins
📊 Track Intake
Aim for 1.2-2g of protein per kg of body weight daily
⏰ Spread Throughout Day
Distribute protein evenly across all meals
💧 Stay Hydrated
Drink plenty of water to support protein metabolism
